# write-linkedin-post

Produces a short, LinkedIn-length draft (a few short paragraphs, not an
article) — different register from `write-article`: a hook first, one
concrete concrete lesson or moment (not a summary of everything), why it
matters, done. No deep technical jargon dump — a LinkedIn feed is
skimmed, not studied.

## Procedure

1. Read `/Users/samrfreitas/Lab/free-wings/.claude/agents/writer.md`
   for the writer's general themes and labeled project-specific
   parallels — the free/open-access-to-technology theme and the
   incremental-testing analogy tend to work particularly well at this
   length; pick whichever genuinely fits the specific moment being
   shared, not all of them.
2. Read the target project's `FOUNDATION.md` plus the one specific
   `LEARNING_LOG.md` entry, `docs/observations/` file, or moment the
   post is actually about — one concrete moment, not a project overview.
3. Write a short draft: a hook (the surprising/human part first, not
   "today I worked on X"), the concrete lesson, why it matters to
   someone who isn't in this project. No links to internal files —
   external readers can't open them.
4. Save the draft to `docs/social/linkedin-<date>.md` in the target
   project, and also show it directly so it's easy to copy.
